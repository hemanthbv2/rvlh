Frequently Asked Questions About Events

❓ Are the academic masterclasses free of charge?

Yes, all entrance masterclasses and open house tours hosted by RV Learning Hub are completely free of charge as part of RSST's educational outreach mission. Prior registration is required to reserve seating.

❓ How do I appear for the RV-TSA Scholarship Exam?

You can register directly on this page or on our admissions portal. The exam is conducted both in physical pen-and-paper mode at all 8 RVLH campuses and in proctored online CBT mode for outstation candidates.

❓ Can parents accompany students to the Harohalli Open House?

Yes! We highly encourage parents to attend. Guided parent walkthroughs of the 50-acre boarding campus, dining halls, medical clinic, and interactive sessions with residential wardens and faculty are organized with complimentary lunch.
