RV-TSA Scholarships & Admissions

Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST) is committed to nurturing merit and academic hunger. Through the RVLH Talent & Scholarship Assessment (RV-TSA), deserving students can earn up to 100% tuition waivers across all 8 PU campuses.

RV-TSA Merit Scholarship Tiers

Scholarship percentages are applied directly onto the tuition fee component for the full 2-year duration.

Platinum Scholar
100% Fee Waiver
Criteria: 96% and above in 10th Board (SSLC / CBSE / ICSE) OR Top 5% rank in the RV-TSA Assessment.
Gold Scholar
50% Fee Waiver
Criteria: 91% to 95.9% in 10th Board OR Top 15% rank in the RV-TSA Assessment.
Silver Scholar
25% Fee Waiver
Criteria: 85% to 90.9% in 10th Board OR Top 30% rank in the RV-TSA Assessment.
Sports & Olympiad
Up to 50% Waiver
Criteria: State or National level representation in recognized sports or National Science/Math Olympiad medalists.

Transparent Fee Guidelines

Unlike external coaching centers that charge separate private tuition fees on top of high school fees, RV Learning Hub offers a unified synchronized package.

  • ✓ Single All-Inclusive Fee: Covers PU Board tuition, complete competitive entrance coaching (JEE / NEET / CA), test series, and study materials.
  • ✓ Flexible Installment Options: Pay conveniently across 2 to 4 scheduled terms throughout the academic year.
  • ✓ Zero-Cost EMI Partnerships: Education loan facilities with leading nationalized banks at 0% effective interest rates.
  • ✓ Hostel & Boarding: Harohalli residential boarding and NMKRV women's hostel fees are billed transparently on a term basis covering 4 meals and laundry.
